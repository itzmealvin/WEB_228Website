**ECOM instruments**, based in the **Federal Republic of Germany**, was established in 1986. It specializes in **explosion-proof mobile devices**, with four main product lines:

- Mobile computers
- Mobile communication devices
- Handheld measuring instruments
- Portable lighting devices

These devices are used across industries such as **oil & gas**, **chemicals**, **petrochemicals**, **mining**, **pharmaceuticals**, **energy**, and **environmental protection**.

Since 2017, **ECOM instruments** has been a subsidiary of the renowned German group **Pepperl+Fuchs**.

---

## Partnership Between 228 Vietnam and ECOM Instruments

Since **2009**, **228 Vietnam (TWO TWO EIGHT)** has partnered with ECOM instruments (Germany) to distribute **explosion-proof products** throughout the Vietnamese market.

Details of this partnership and distributor exclusivity are publicly listed on **ECOM's official website**.

## Guaranteed Quality & Certification

Purchasing ECOM products from 228 Vietnam ensures:

- **100% authentic and certified products**
- Full support for **certificates of origin (C/O)**, **quality certificates (C/Q)**, and related documents upon delivery

Importantly, **ECOM's mobile phone models** have been **certified by the Authority of Telecommunications – Ministry of Information and Communications (MIC)** to meet Vietnam's **GSM network regulatory standards**.

## Manufacturer's Safety Advisory

To ensure **maximum operational safety**, all explosion-proof equipment—after a certain period of use—**must be sent back to the manufacturer** for inspection and revalidation of its **original ATEX/IECEx certification**, as outlined in each product's maintenance guidelines.

## Our Recommendation to Customers

We strongly advise customers to choose **new products with clear origin and official certification**, including **Conformity and Technical Regulations (QCVN/TCVN)** approved by the **Ministry of Information and Communications**, to guarantee safety for **personnel, assets, and operational environments**.
