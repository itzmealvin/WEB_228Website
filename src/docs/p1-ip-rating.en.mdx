## 📘 What is an IP Rating?

**IP** stands for **Ingress Protection Rating** (or **International Protection Rating**). It defines a device's ability to resist **intrusion from solid particles and liquids**, critical for electronics and industrial devices.

Typical formats include: `IPX1X2` or `IPA0X1X2B0`  
Where:

- `X1` and `X2` are protection digits
- `A0` and `B0` are optional letter codes

## 🅰️ Additional IP Letters

| Letter | Meaning                               |
| ------ | ------------------------------------- |
| W      | Weather-resistant (rain, wind, heat)  |
| S      | Device inactive during water exposure |
| M      | Device active during water exposure   |
