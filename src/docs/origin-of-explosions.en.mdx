## 🔺 Fire Triangle

Any **fire** requires three essential elements, known as the **Fire Triangle**:

1. **Oxygen:** Air or an oxygen source
2. **Ignition Source:** Heat, spark, electrical discharge, etc.
3. **Fuel:** Combustible material such as gasoline, gas, dust...

## 💥 Explosion Hexagon

An **explosion** requires three additional factors beyond the fire triangle, forming the **Explosion Hexagon**:

4. **Gas or Dust in Suspension:** Gas or dust particles suspended in the air
5. **Concentration:** Sufficient concentration of these particles or gases
6. **Explosive Limits:** Minimum and maximum limits at which explosion can occur

> **Technical Notes:**
>
> - _Suspension_ refers to the airborne distribution of particles or vapors
> - _Concentration_ is the amount of fuel present in a given volume
> - _Explosive limits_ define the concentration thresholds within which ignition causes an explosion

## 🧯 Fire and Explosion Prevention

- If **any side of the fire triangle is missing**, a fire cannot start.
- If **one element of the explosion hexagon is removed**, the explosion will not occur.

> 💡 **All fire protection strategies are based on eliminating at least one element from the Fire Triangle or Explosion Hexagon.**
